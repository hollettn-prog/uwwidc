Licensing Road Trip Debrief

Know Where the Answer Lives.

State Agency

Start with the official educator licensing authority.

Your Pathway

Out-of-state, reciprocity and endorsement pathways may differ.

Requirements

Tests, fingerprints, applications and documentation can change.

Verify

Don't pay, register or submit based on somebody else's experience.

The goal isn't memorizing licensing rules.
Know what applies to you, what you need to verify, and where the official answer lives.
