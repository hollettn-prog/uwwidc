Nikki's Preseason Briefing

The First Few Weeks

You are not supposed to feel completely ready.

  • You are learning a school, students, routines and people.
  • You may feel confident one day and completely lost the next.
  • Your teaching load and responsibility will grow.
  • Your questions should become more specific over time.
  • You are here to learn — not prove that you already know everything.

🔒 Instructor Notes

Time: about 5 minutes.

Use one short personal/student-teacher example if helpful. Normalize uncertainty without implying that serious concerns should simply be tolerated.

Listen for later: students who describe being given inappropriate responsibility, no support, or serious placement concerns.

Student Teaching Survival Guide

🧰 Things I Wish Every Student Teacher Knew

1. Ask before assuming.

2. Communicate earlier than feels necessary.

3. Your cooperating teacher does not expect you to know everything.

4. Write things down.

5. Save professional evidence as you go.

6. A bad lesson is data, not a verdict.

7. Don't wait until you're drowning to tell someone you're struggling.

8. Protect student and placement privacy.

9. Use your people.

🔒 Instructor Notes

Game Situation Debrief

Professional Response

1️⃣ Communicate Early

Don't wait until after the school day begins.

2️⃣ Contact Your CT

Tell the person expecting you at school.

3️⃣ Follow School Procedures

Contact the office or follow district procedures when required.

4️⃣ Notify Your Supervisor

Keep your University Supervisor informed.

5️⃣ Protect Tomorrow's Teaching

Make sure needed plans and materials are available.

6️⃣ Submit the Absence Report

Complete the PEPROF Student Teaching Absence Report.

Simple rule: Communicate early. Take care of your responsibilities. Keep the appropriate people informed.
