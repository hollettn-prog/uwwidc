Interaction 2

🏟️ Who Would You Call First?

Situation A

A student consistently struggles to access an activity despite several adaptations you've tried.

Situation B

Your CT gives you feedback that you don't completely understand or agree with.

Situation C

You're unsure whether something a student disclosed requires an official response.

Situation D

You're comparing two job opportunities and don't know what questions you should be asking.

For each:

  • Who might you contact?
  • Why that person?
  • What would you ask?
08:00
Debrief

The Right Person Depends on the Problem.

CT / Colleague

Immediate teaching context and practical experience.

Specialist

Special educator, APE professional, counselor, nurse, psychologist, OT/PT or another relevant professional.

Supervisor / Administrator

Placement, policy, serious concerns and situations requiring responsibility or authority.

Professional Network

Career decisions, resources, mentorship and another perspective.

Professional judgment isn't knowing every answer.
Sometimes it's recognizing that the answer belongs with someone else.
