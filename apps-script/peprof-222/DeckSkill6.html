PEPROF 2224
STRATEGIC PROXIMITY

Position changes can support participation quietly

Where would you stand—and where would you move next?

Keep the whole group in view while supporting an individual or hotspot.

PEPROF 2225
SUPPORT LADDER

Begin with the least intrusive response likely to work

1Reinforce appropriate participation
2Use proximity or a nonverbal cue
3Give a brief redirection
4Reteach or adjust the task
PEPROF 2226
LANGUAGE MATTERS

Make the expected action easier to perform

Instead of

“How many times do I have to tell you to stop messing around?”

Try

“Keep the ball still while directions are being given. Thank you.”

PEPROF 2227
WITHITNESS SIMULATOR

Choose a response without unnecessarily stopping activity

What did you notice first? Where would you move? What would you say—if anything?

Explain why your response is proportionate.

PEPROF 2228
RESPONSE REHEARSAL

Notice → position → cue → follow through

Calm tone
Few words
Private when possible
Protect dignity
Return to participation
