Your Teaching Toolkit

Throughout your methods courses, you will create resources that can support your planning and teaching.

This toolkit gives you one place to develop, organize, and revisit that work as you prepare for student teaching. I encourage you to treat it as a working collection. Return to resources as your thinking develops, revise them when you learn something new, and keep the pieces that will be useful to you in the classroom.
