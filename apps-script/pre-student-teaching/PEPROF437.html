Elementary Physical Education

The resources in this toolkit are designed to help you understand elementary learners, think through important teaching decisions, and create materials you can return to during field experiences and student teaching.

Module 1 · Foundations of Elementary PE
My Elementary PE Philosophy
Think through what matters to you as an elementary PE teacher and how those beliefs might show up in your teaching.
Available
Module 2 · Understanding Elementary Learners
Child Development Quick Reference Guide
Create a practical reference for physical, cognitive, social, and emotional development, then use what you know about development to make instructional decisions.
Available
Module 3 · Curriculum & Planning
Curriculum Planning Resource
Build a year-at-a-glance elementary PE curriculum and explain the decisions behind what you teach, when you teach it, and what you prioritize.
Available
These resources are meant to grow with you. As you gain experience in schools, come back to them, make changes, and adapt them to the students and teaching situations you encounter.
