Curriculum Planning Resource

In this activity, you'll make some of the same choices elementary PE teachers make when planning a curriculum: what deserves instructional time, when it makes sense to teach it, and what to adjust when the year does not go exactly as planned.

BUILD YOUR YEAR

When would you teach it?

Build a simple September–May/June curriculum map. You can choose up to two areas per month.

You do not need to use every priority only once. If something is important enough to revisit, you can include it more than once.

A few planning reminders
  • Some skills provide a foundation for later learning.
  • Important content may need to be revisited.
  • Weather and available space can affect timing.
  • Not every unit needs the same amount of time.
  • Your sequence should make sense for learning, not just the calendar.
EXPLAIN THREE DECISIONS

Why does your year look the way it does?

Choose three decisions from your curriculum that you could explain to another PE teacher. Keep your responses focused. A few thoughtful sentences for each is enough.

1. Sequence

Think about something you intentionally placed before or after something else.

Show me an example

I put throwing and catching earlier in the year because those skills are used again in several activities later in the curriculum.

2. Instructional Time

Think about something that deserves more or less instructional time than another area.

Show me an example

I gave fundamental movement skills more time than basketball because those skills transfer across many activities and provide a foundation for later learning.

3. Timing

Think about why one part of your curriculum makes sense at a particular point in the school year.

Show me an example

I scheduled track and field in May because outdoor space gives students more room for running, jumping, and throwing activities.

A CHANGE OF PLANS

Your gym is unavailable for three weeks

Curriculum plans often need to change once the school year begins. Consider how you would adjust your plan without simply giving up three weeks of learning.

The situation

Your principal lets you know that the gym will be unavailable for three weeks because of testing and school events.

You still have PE during that time, but you will need to adjust your curriculum and available space.

REVIEW YOUR CURRICULUM

Take one look at the whole year

Before you save your map, look at the year as a whole. Does the sequence make sense? Are your priorities visible? Could you explain the major choices you made?
