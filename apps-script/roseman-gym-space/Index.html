Planning prompts and source notes

Mark the actual entrances/exits and fixed obstacles first. Show movement direction, equipment collection, waiting areas and the teacher’s position. Explain how every student can reach each activity. Review your plan with your instructor before setting it up.

Optional challenge: Plan four stations for 28 students, including a student using a wheelchair. Show transitions and an accessible route to every station. Identify one change you would make if a station becomes crowded.
